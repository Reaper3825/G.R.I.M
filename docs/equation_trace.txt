DIAGNOSIS: WHY project_out_pc1 DELAYED BUT DID NOT PREVENT COLLAPSE
================================================================================

THE KEY INSIGHT — PC1 IS NOT THE ROOT CAUSE, IT'S A SYMPTOM:

  1. project_out_pc1 removes the dominant direction from h BEFORE the LM head.
     This means the LM head sees decorrelated hidden states (avg_cos ≈ 0.05).

  2. BUT: the encoder STILL produces ρ=0.992 correlated hidden states.
     The PC1 projection is a POST-HOC fix — it doesn't change what the
     encoder learns.

  3. The ENCODER gradients flow through (I - gg^T), which is a rank-(D-1)
     projection. This removes the PC1 component from dL/dh before it reaches
     the encoder. The encoder effectively receives:
       grad_encoder = (I - gg^T) * grad_logits @ W^T
     This REMOVES gradient signal along the dominant direction, preventing
     the encoder from learning to differentiate along that axis.

  4. Result: encoder learns even MORE aggressively along the NON-PC1 directions.
     Since avg_cos measures ALL directions, the encoder saturates at ρ=0.992
     regardless of PC1 removal.

MATHEMATICAL PROOF — WHY LOGIT RANGE SHRINKS:

  The LM head sees: h̃[t] = h[t] - (h[t]·g)*g
  
  At batch 10, PC1 explains 99.9% of variance:
    ||h[t]||² ≈ (h[t]·g)² + ε    where ε = 0.1% of total variance
    ||h̃[t]||² ≈ ε × ||h[t]||²   << ||h[t]||²

  logit range ~ ||h̃|| × ||W|| × (spread of cosines)
  
  As PC1 absorbs more variance:
    ||h̃|| → 0, logit range → 0

  MEASURED: logit_max shrinks from 1.57 (batch 1) to 0.18 (batch 10).
  This is CORRECT behavior of the projection — but it also means the
  model can't express preferences through logits!

  The projection creates a PARADOX:
    - It decorrelates h̃ (avg_cos → 0.05) ✓
    - But it also crushes ||h̃|| (projection onto (D-1)-dim subspace) ✗
    - Logits become near-uniform → softmax entropy stays at ln(V)
    - Loss barely decreases: 9.27 → 9.20 (vs 9.27 → 7.20 without PC1)

WHY TOKEN 36 STILL EMERGES (SLOWER):

  Even after PC1 removal, the (D-1)-dimensional residual h̃ still has
  structure. The SECOND principal component (PC2) becomes the new dominant
  direction. As the encoder aligns h more, PC2 also becomes aligned, and
  W[36] has the highest projection onto PC2.

  The feedback loop is the SAME, just operating in the (D-1)-dim subspace:
    logit[36] = h̃ · W[36]^T → still largest because W[36] grows
    → grad makes W[36] grow more → logit[36] still wins

  Removing PC1 is like removing one head of a hydra — more grow back.

CONCLUSION:

  project_out_pc1 is INSUFFICIENT because:
  1. It doesn't prevent the encoder from building correlated representations
  2. It crushes logit magnitude (||h̃|| → 0 as PC1 absorbs variance)
  3. The collapse mechanism operates in ALL rank-k subspaces, not just PC1
  4. Loss improvement is negligible (0.7% vs 22% without PC1)

  The ROOT CAUSE is the encoder's tendency to produce aligned hidden states
  through 12 layers of causal attention + shared weights. The fix must be
  INSIDE the encoder (e.g., per-layer decorrelation, spectral normalization,
  or architectural changes to break the alignment feedback loop).

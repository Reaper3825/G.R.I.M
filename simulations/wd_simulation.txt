8. THE CORE PROBLEM: WEIGHT DECAY IS GLOBAL, THE BUG IS LOCAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Weight decay applies uniformly to every row of the embedding matrix.
  The tok36 problem is a PER-ROW gradient magnitude problem.

  Tok36 receives 2.0× the gradient push of a typical content token.
  To suppress tok36 to content-token level, decay must exceed tok36's
  gradient push. But that same decay also exceeds content tokens'
  gradient push, actively destroying them.

  There is NO wd value that simultaneously:
    (a) constrains tok36 W_rms to ≤ 0.030
    (b) does not shrink content tokens below their current W_rms

  The crossover point is wd ≈ 0.75:
    - Below 0.75: content tokens still grow, tok36 barely constrained
    - Above 0.75: content tokens shrink, tok36 constrained but at cost of model capacity

  At the "sweet spot" wd = 0.5:
    - tok36 ends at 0.035 (vs 0.045 actual — 22% reduction)
    - content ends at 0.036 (vs 0.033 actual — 9% increase)
    - But this does NOT fix argmax dominance. Tok36's dominance comes from its
      GRADIENT SIGNAL being 2× stronger, not merely its weight norm being 1.34×.
      Weight norm is a symptom. Reducing it via global decay treats the symptom.

  INCREASING GLOBAL WEIGHT DECAY WILL NOT FIX THE TOK36 COLLAPSE.

  Quantitative basis:
  1. Current wd=0.01 removed 0.70% of tok36's growth. Ineffective.
  2. wd=0.5 would reduce tok36 from 0.045 to 0.035 (22% lower) but
     simultaneously inflate content tokens from 0.033 to 0.036 (9% higher).
     Net disparity reduced from 1.34× to 0.99× — but this is cosmetic.
  3. wd=1.0 constrains tok36 to 0.032 but SHRINKS content tokens to 0.030.
     The disparity PERSISTS at 1.07× while the model loses capacity.
  4. wd=1.69 (needed to cap tok36 at 0.030) causes 24% shrinkage of content
     tokens. Catastrophically destructive.
  5. No global wd value satisfies both constraints simultaneously.

  The root cause is tok36 receiving 2× the per-step gradient push
  of content tokens. Global weight decay cannot selectively penalize
  one embedding row. Weight norm is a downstream symptom of gradient
  flow asymmetry, not the cause of argmax dominance.
